Features

Everything your brain needs

Built on the intersection of AI, psychology, and great design — to actually work the way your mind does.

🧠

AI Context Memory New

Unlike basic reminder apps, NexMem builds a persistent model of you — your preferences, habits, relationships, and goals. The longer you use it, the smarter it gets. It remembers that you prefer morning meetings, that "the client" means Acme Corp, and that you've been trying to drink more water.

🔔

Smart Reminders

Set reminders with natural language. Recurring, one-time, or context-triggered. "Remind me about this when I'm near the grocery store."

☀️

Daily Briefings

Wake up to a personalized morning briefing — your meetings, tasks, weather context, and a motivational memory from your past all in one card.

✨

Serendipity Engine New

Our AI periodically resurfaces your old notes, ideas, and memories at moments when they become newly relevant. Like déjà vu, but useful.

📋

Second Brain Notes

Go beyond reminders. Capture long-form thoughts, ideas, and knowledge. NexMem links related notes automatically, building your personal knowledge base.

🎯

Focus Modes New

Activate Deep Work, Flow State, or Recovery mode. NexMem adjusts notification frequency, surfaces only what matters, and tracks your focus hours.

📅

Calendar Sync

Connects with Google Calendar, Outlook, and Apple Calendar. NexMem understands your schedule and plans around your existing commitments automatically.

👥

Friend Reminders

Send accountability reminders to friends or receive gentle nudges on their behalf. Stay connected without the awkward nagging conversations.

😊

Mood & Energy Tracking New

Log your daily mood and energy levels. NexMem correlates this data with your schedule and activities to help you discover your personal peak performance patterns.
